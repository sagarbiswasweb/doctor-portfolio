How We Treat Hernias

Advanced surgical services for hernia and related abdominal wall conditions

Laparoscopic Hernia Surgery

Minimally invasive repair for inguinal, umbilical, and femoral hernias with faster recovery and minimal scarring.

Open Hernia Surgery

Traditional repair technique for complicated, large, or recurrent hernias ensuring safe and effective treatment.

Pediatric & Specialized Hernia Care

Expert care for children, recurrent hernias, parastomal hernias, and complex abdominal wall hernias.
